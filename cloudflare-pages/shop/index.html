How purchasing works

  • Photography prints stay on bitterbuffaloart.com
  • Tie-dye and laser listings are added only when an individual Etsy URL is already verified
  • Original paintings and the two new layered wood pieces stay as inquiries until each one has its own verified Etsy listing URL. An inquiry link is not an Etsy listing.
  • Active tie-dye garments, engraved cutting boards, decorative wood rounds, and wood coaster sets are linked to their own Etsy listings. The shop-home address is not a buy link.
  • This website does not take payment

The verified Etsy shop home is etsy.com/shop/BitterBuffaloArt. That address is the shop, not a listing for a photograph, garment, or wood piece.
