Print pricing

  • The public selectors offer premium framed canvas, aluminum metal, and archival matte, at 12×18, 16×24, and 20×30, only when that file is within 0.5 percent of 3:2 and meets the PPI minimum. Stretched canvas, semi-gloss, and Somerset Velvet stay in the pricing record and are not sale options.
  • 16×24 premium 1.5-inch framed canvas is $199.33 when the file qualifies. Ordering is by inquiry. This page does not take payment.
  • A buy control appears on a print page only when that material and size has a verified Square Payment Link. A Payment Link takes payment for the print price. It does not place a LumaPrints order.
  • A size that would crop the photograph is omitted. A size below the PPI minimum is disabled. Acrylic is not shown.
  • Four photographs stay 16:9 and are not cropped: “Small Life, Wide Silence,” “Where Stone Holds the Stars,” “Gold Beneath the Wild,” and “The Gold That Stayed.” No price is published for them.

Send a print inquiry. Handmade tie-dye and laser work remain separate from these photographs.
