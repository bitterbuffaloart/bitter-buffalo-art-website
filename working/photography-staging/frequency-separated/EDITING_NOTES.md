# Bitter Buffalo Art — Frequency-separated tone mapped photographs

Seven distinct uploaded photos were tonally edited. The original JPEGs are not overwritten. The two exact repeat subjects (DSC_6980 cabin, published #25; DSC_9301 elk silhouette, published #48) were deliberately omitted from this edit set.

## Method

1. Convert the 8-bit original color image to CIE Lab.
2. Decompose luminance into smooth low-frequency tones (Gaussian-blurred L, sigma=27 pixels) and high-frequency detail (original L minus smooth L).
3. Apply restrained, feathered highlight and shadow remapping to the low-frequency band only.
4. Reconstruct with the original high-frequency layer, applying minimal midtone-only detail contrast.
5. Preserve framing, dimensions and scene content. Export high-quality JPEG with available original EXIF.

This is real computational frequency separation and does not generate replacement sky, animals, or artificial detail. The uploaded files are 2048px on the long edge, not verified high-resolution print masters.

## Editorial choices

Publish candidates only after integrating edited files into the website source and verifying master suitability for each print configuration. The three new selections have proposed display positions 51–53 and distinct permanent IDs bb-photo-52 to bb-photo-54, since bb-photo-45 belongs to the bison calf and bb-photo-46 through bb-photo-51 belong to the last six additions.
