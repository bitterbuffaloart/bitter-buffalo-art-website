About engraved aspen rounds

The buffalo piece is a decorative aspen round, 14×8.5 in, for tabletop or wall display. The fox, Elk Trail, and cracked elk photographs are decorative engraved wood rounds. Free wall-hanger installation is available upon customer request.

Cutting boards

The cutting boards are separate from the rounds. Solar Summit, Moonrise Ridge, Celestial Trail, and Alpine Orbit are documented as engraved wood cutting boards. Celestial Trail is the paddle board. Each listing is the matching Etsy page for that photographed piece.
