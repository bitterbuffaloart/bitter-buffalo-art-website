About Bitter Buffalo Art

Bitter Buffalo Art is a Colorado studio working in photography, dyed cloth, laser and wood, and the sketchbook.

The studio practice is Colorado photography, ice-dye and reverse-dye clothing, laser-engraved and layered wood, illuminated work, and the working sketchbook. Natural materials are chosen and finished with care.

Photography prints are offered on this website. This page does not take payment.
